Prayer times add-on for Oyenza Studio: what it holds and where it comes from

places.json
  Every location in Kemenag RI's (Bimas Islam) prayer schedule, named as
  Kemenag writes them, each with a reference point fitted to Kemenag's
  published 2026 schedule; plus Makkah, Madinah and Jeddah (coordinates
  from en.wikipedia.org). Part of Oyenza Studio, under its MIT license.

adzan.m4a
  "The Adhan - Muslim Call to Prayer - Aaqib Azeez" by Atcovi, from
  Wikimedia Commons (the version uploaded on 11 December 2025):
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Adhan_-_Muslim_Call_to_Prayer_-_Aaqib_Azeez.mp3

  Changes: made mono, the rumble below 70 Hz filtered out, its loudness
  levelled (a few peaks limited), faded in and out, and saved as AAC
  (80 kbit/s). This adzan.m4a is shared under the same license,
