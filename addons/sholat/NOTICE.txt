Prayer times add-on for Oyenza Studio: what it holds and where it comes from

places.json
  Every location in Kemenag RI's (Bimas Islam) prayer schedule, named as
  Kemenag writes them, each with a reference point fitted to Kemenag's
  published 2026 schedule; plus Makkah, Madinah and Jeddah (coordinates
  from en.wikipedia.org). Part of Oyenza Studio, under its MIT license.

world.tsv.zst
  Cities of 15,000 people or more outside Indonesia, from GeoNames
  (https://www.geonames.org), as packed in the geonamescache package 3.0.2.
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: Indonesia left out (places.json holds Kemenag's own places), and
  the places within 15 km of Makkah, Madinah and Jeddah; points rounded to
  three decimals; each country's prayer-time method added. The time zones'
  offsets, 2026 to 2045, come from the IANA time zone database (public
  domain).

adzan.m4a
  "Maghrib Adhan at the Masjid al Haram, Mecca - 25 Feb, 2012" by
  3omar Faruq (YouTube: yodasdad), from Wikimedia Commons:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maghrib_Adhan_at_the_Masjid_al_Haram,_Mecca_-_25_Feb,_2012.webm
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Changes: only the adzan kept (1:54 to 4:52 of the video), made mono,
  faded in and out, its loudness levelled, and saved as AAC (80 kbit/s).
