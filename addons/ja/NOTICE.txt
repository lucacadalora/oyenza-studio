Japanese readings add-on for Oyen Poni: what it holds and where it comes from

ja-readings.dic.zst
  Oyen Poni's own Japanese reading dictionary: romaji under kanji in
  Japanese lyrics on PCs where Windows has no Japanese reading data. Built
  by tools/make_ja_dict.py from mecab-ipadic (below), compiled for vibrato
  and packed with zstd. The same file is crates/poni-core/data/ja-readings.dic.zst
  in https://github.com/lucacadalora/poni.

mecab-ipadic 2.7.0-20070801

Used for: Oyen Poni's own Japanese reading dictionary,
crates/poni-core/data/ja-readings.dic.zst. It is a modified copy, made by
tools/make_ja_dict.py: trimmed for lyrics (person and company names and rare
place names left out, each entry cut to its word class and reading) and
compiled for vibrato.

Source: https://deb.debian.org/debian/pool/main/m/mecab-ipadic/mecab-ipadic_2.7.0-20070801+main.orig.tar.gz
