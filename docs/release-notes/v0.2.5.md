**Your steps, on your computer.** In the Oyenza app on your phone, turn on **Show on Oyenza Studio** under your steps in Health (Sehat), and the island's Health page shows your daily steps, distance and active energy for the last 7 days, with a notice when you pass your goal. It asks every 15 minutes and when you open the Health page, and keeps the numbers in memory only. Sleep, heart rate and weight stay on your phone. You need to be signed in to Oyenza on the computer, and the newest Oyenza app on your phone. This works on Windows and on the Mac.

**More of Windows on the Mac:**
- **Sign in with Oyenza** works on the Mac now: approve the code at oyenza.app/connect, and the Oyenza tab shows your last 7 days and the bills due. Money in and out arrives as a notice, with the sum held back until you click **Show**, and each bill gets a reminder the day before. The menu bar has an Oyenza menu too. The Mac's key stays in a file only your Mac account can read.
- **Full-screen apps:** while a video, a game or a slideshow is full screen, the island now steps out of its way and comes back after, as on Windows. This is the new default on the Mac. To keep it on top, pick **Stay on top** under **In full-screen apps** in the menu bar or in Settings.
- **Headphone pop-ups:** connecting or disconnecting Bluetooth headphones or a speaker shows the same pop-up as on Windows, without the battery level (macOS shows AirPods' own when they connect).
- **Power:** on a Mac without a battery (a Mac mini, Studio, iMac or Mac Pro with Apple silicon), System › Power shows what the whole Mac draws, from its own sensor. MacBooks keep their Battery view.
- Settings says **Start at login** on the Mac, not "Start with Windows".

The Mac download grows by about 230 KB, to 3.9 MB.

**The version in the menu.** Right-click the island or the tray icon: the menu now shows **About Oyenza Studio 0.2.5** and its date, just above Quit, and it opens these notes. The tray icon's tooltip names the version too.

Everything new in 0.2.4 is here too: over a maximized browser, the island steps aside for your tabs. See the [0.2.4 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.4).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading:** run the new installer. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
