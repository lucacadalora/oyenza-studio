**Slide Diorama's glass ball.** Press the glass ball on Diorama's resting pill and slide it along, like a day and night switch. The place runs on through the day with it: by day through the golden hour and the sunset into the night, as it will be 45 minutes after sunset; at night through the small hours into the morning, as it will be 50 minutes after sunrise.

- Let go and the ball rolls on to the end it was nearer, or the one you threw it toward. Tap it to send it to the other end.
- Left on the other side of the day, the whole theme keeps that side until the clock comes round to it, then follows the sun again.
- Pressing the ball never opens or moves the island, and a pointer resting on it doesn't open it either. Press anywhere else on the island for that.

**The whole island matches.** In Diorama, the open card, the music, timer and volume pills and the notices are the resting pill's raised frame round a pane now: cream with dark slate ink by day, indigo with cream ink and the window's gold at night, in the Outfit typeface, with a warm glow for a shadow at night. They change with the ball. Their pages and sizes are Pill's.

**As long as a notch.** The resting pill is 190 by 38 DIPs whatever it shows (it was 80 to 124 wide), a little longer than a MacBook's notch, so the ball has a long way to slide. The garden fence is longer, with two bushes and a lamp post that's lit from dusk. In Advanced settings, Diorama's card paints the place on a shorter pane, so it still fits.

Nothing new runs at rest. The picture is still painted once a minute, with the tick the clock already wakes for. A slide draws a frame each time the pointer moves while the ball is in hand, and the ball's roll to its end draws frames for a second or so.

Classic stays the default.

Everything new in 0.2.28 is here too: Prayer times can follow Muhammadiyah, with Majelis Tarjih's times and the Kalender Hijriah Global Tunggal (KHGT), and today's Hijri date shows above the arc. See the [0.2.28 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.28).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.28, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
