**Caffeine: what you drink, and your last call for coffee.** Caffeine is an add-on that's built in, so there's nothing to download: open the **+** at the end of the tabs and switch it on. Its **Caffeine** tab shows how much caffeine is still in you, hour by hour, from 06:00 to 06:00.

- **Add a drink by its kind and shots.** An espresso drink (Americano, Latte, Es kopi susu, Cappuccino, Espresso, Flat white or Mocha) counts the shots that went in, and its beans: Arabica (75 mg a shot), Blend (105), Robusta (120) or Decaf (15). Coffee chains in Indonesia don't publish their shots, so the count is yours to enter. Kopi tubruk, Instant coffee, Kopi sachet, Filter coffee, Cold brew, Tea, Green tea, Energy drink and Cola count by the spoon, sachet, cup, bottle or can, and **Your own** takes a name and its mg.
- **Change its time.** A drink's time starts at now. Change it before you add the drink, or later by dragging its dot on the chart.
- **See your last call.** The page shows the latest time today your usual drink can go in and leave at most 35 mg in you at bedtime. The 35 mg follows a 2023 review of 24 studies of caffeine and sleep, which gives a 107 mg coffee at least 8.8 hours before bed and a 217.5 mg drink at least 13.2 hours: both leave about 35 mg at bedtime. With bed at 23:00, a 2-shot Americano's last call is about 12:07.
- **Get the reminder.** Thirty minutes before last call, the island says **Last call for coffee**, and under it: "After that, more than 35 mg is left at 23:00".
- **Settings:** your bedtime (23:00 until you change it), your half-life (5 hours, the average), the reminder, and the day limit. The page warns past 400 mg in a day, the FDA's and EFSA's figure for a healthy adult.

Every number is an average for that kind of drink and for an average body, not a reading of you. What each one is and where it comes from: [docs/caffeine.md](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/blob/main/docs/caffeine.md). Starbucks publishes 75 mg for its Signature shot; the 15 mg that goes around is its Short decaf brewed coffee.

Your drinks stay on your computer, two weeks of them in `data.json`, and nothing is sent anywhere. Nothing runs at rest for Caffeine but its reminder, once a day while it's on. Switched off, there's no tab and no reminder.

Caffeine makes poni.exe about 170 KB bigger (10.0 MB) and the download about 50 KB (3.3 MB).

Everything new in 0.2.28 is here too: Prayer times can follow Muhammadiyah, with the KHGT Hijri date. See the [0.2.28 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.28).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.29, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
