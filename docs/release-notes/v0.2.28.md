**Prayer times: Kemenag or Muhammadiyah.** Prayer times can follow Muhammadiyah now, as well as Kemenag. Open the Prayer tab, click **Change place**, and pick under **Times and Hijri dates**:

- **Kemenag**, as before: Kemenag RI's schedule, and the government's Hijri calendar (MABIMS, with the months the sidang isbat announced).
- **Muhammadiyah (KHGT)**: Majelis Tarjih's schedule for places in Indonesia, with Subuh at 18° (about 8 minutes after Kemenag's), and the Kalender Hijriah Global Tunggal, one Hijri date for the whole world. Its times match khgt.muhammadiyah.or.id to the minute, or within one. Its dates are the months Muhammadiyah published up to 1451 H.

The two calendars often begin a month a day apart: Ramadan 1447 started on 18 February 2026 by KHGT and on 19 February by the government. Cities outside Indonesia keep their own country's times either way; the choice gives them the calendar.

**Today's Hijri date** shows above the arc on the Prayer page, such as "1 Jumadil Akhir 1448 H", by the calendar you follow. Where the island is narrow, the qibla's compass point makes room for it. While you follow Muhammadiyah, its name stands beside your place.

Nothing new runs at rest. The date is worked out from the moon itself, once a month, in a few milliseconds: no download, nothing online. poni.exe grows by 30 KB.

Everything new in 0.2.27 is here too: Diorama, a fifth look, whose resting pill is a small window onto a pixel-art place that keeps your day. See the [0.2.27 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.27).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.27, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
