**Diorama, a fifth theme.** Right-click the island, then **Theme › Diorama**, or pick it under Theme in Settings; on a Mac, it's in the menu bar's Theme menu too. Diorama is the Pill theme with a picture in its resting capsule: a small window onto a pixel-art place, with a house at one end, a tree at the other and the time written in the sky between them.

- It follows your real time. Dawn is pink, the day is blue with the sun crossing the sky, the light turns golden from about an hour before sunset, and after dusk the stars come out under the moon in tonight's phase. The window is lit until midnight and again from five, and an orange cat keeps the same hours: under the tree by day, home for the golden hour, in the window after dark.
- A glass ball rests on the house by day and on the tree at night. At sunset it rolls across and night falls behind it; at sunrise it rolls back. It also rolls once when you pick the theme.
- With the Prayer times add-on on and a place picked, the sun rises and sets when it does there. Otherwise it rises at six and sets at six.
- The time is 12- or 24-hour as you've set it. On a laptop, a bolt stands beside it while the battery charges, and a red battery when it's low. Its idle looks are **Clock**, **Minimal**, which shows the place without the clock, and **Hidden until hover**. It has no eyes and no status bar: a setting left on Eyes from another theme shows the place alone, and one left on Status bar shows it with the clock.
- Everything else is Pill's: music, timers, pop-ups, notices and the open card.

**On a MacBook with a notch**, Pill rests inside the notch, where a picture would never be seen. Diorama's resting pill floats just below the notch instead, and stays where you drag it.

**In Advanced settings**, the five theme cards sit in two rows now, and Diorama's card shows its picture.

Nothing new runs at rest. The picture is painted once a minute, with the tick the clock already wakes for, and kept until the next. Only the ball's roll draws frames, for about a second and a half.

Classic stays the default.

Everything new in 0.2.26 is here too: the Add-ons tab shows what each Studio Pro feature does, AI usage is part of Studio Pro, and Claude's Max 20x plan reads Ultra 10x. See the [0.2.26 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.26).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.26, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
