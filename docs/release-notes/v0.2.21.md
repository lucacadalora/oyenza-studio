**Settings in the island again.** The gear on the open island opens Settings inside the island again, as it did up to 0.2.17: your Oyenza account, the theme and idle look, the switches you use most, the version with **Check now**, and the tabs. Its last row, **Advanced settings**, opens the window with every setting, and the island closes as the window opens, so the two never sit on top of each other. In the tray menu and the Mac's menu bar item, the window's line is now **Advanced settings…** (still ⌘, on a Mac). When a new version is out, **Update** sits in the version's row, where Check now is.

Everything new in 0.2.20 and 0.2.19 is here too: the Settings window that glides at your screen's own rate, the Downloader's progress, and Meeting notes and the IELTS coach on the Mac. See the [0.2.20 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.20).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.20, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
