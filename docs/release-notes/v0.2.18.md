**A Settings window.** The gear on the open island now opens every setting in one place, in a window of its own, and the island stays the island. A sidebar takes you through General, Appearance, Island, Music and lyrics, Pop-ups, Tabs, Add-ons, Focus, Prayer times, AI usage, your Oyenza account and About. It follows your system's light or dark mode, and changes apply at once. On Windows, **Settings…** in the tray menu opens it too, and Esc closes it. On a Mac it's a real Mac window, with the title bar buttons over its sidebar: **Settings…** in the menu bar item (⌘,) opens it, and ⌘W closes it.

**Settings you couldn't reach before.** How soon the island opens when you point at it (Quick, Normal or Slow) and how soon it closes, how wide it is when open, the display it hangs on, the volume, charger and Bluetooth pop-ups, which page it opens on, the 24-hour clock, the song on the closed island, the lyrics' timing, whether breaks and focus sessions start by themselves, pausing music for the adzan, and when the AI tab warns you about a limit. Until now these lived only in settings.json.

**The Downloader on the Mac (Studio Pro, early access).** The Downloads tab comes to the Mac, on macOS 14 and later, Apple silicon and Intel: paste a link, pick **Video** or **Sound**, and press **Download**. Switching the add-on on downloads its tools once, each checked by size and SHA-256. Download only what you have the right to keep.

Everything new in 0.2.17 and 0.2.16 is here too: the Downloader on Windows, IELTS for Studio Pro, the AI tab, Meeting notes and OK on the prayer countdown. See the [0.2.17 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.17).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.17, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
