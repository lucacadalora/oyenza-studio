**A clearer adzan in Prayer times.** The adzan is a new recording, clean and full, without the crowd, the echo and the phone-call sound of the old one: "The Adhan - Muslim Call to Prayer - Aaqib Azeez" by Atcovi, from Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 4.0 (the credit and licence come with the add-on, in `NOTICE.txt`). The Prayer times add-on is 959 KB now instead of 1.9 MB. If you have it on, it downloads the new files once after the update, checked against the fingerprint built into the app, and deletes the old ones.

**Antigravity on the AI tab, on a Mac.** Antigravity (`agy`), which took over from Gemini CLI, gets a card: the 5-hour and weekly limits of each group of models, Gemini, Claude and GPT. The AI tab asks `agy` itself, through its own `/usage` command, which uses none of your quota: only while the AI tab is open, and at most every 15 minutes. It needs `agy` 1.1.11 or later. It never opens a browser: if `agy` wants you to sign in again, the card says so; sign in to Antigravity, then press **Refresh** on the AI tab. On Windows, the AI tab doesn't read Antigravity yet. Thanks to itzmail (#192).

**Notifications isn't an add-on any more.** Windows lets only the Microsoft Store copy of an app read other apps' notifications, so in the download from GitHub, Notifications' switch in Add-ons could only open a tab that said so. Add-ons doesn't list it now. Where it can work, in the Store copy, Notifications is on from the start, reads nothing until you press **Allow** and Windows says yes, and switches off under **Tabs** in Settings.

**On Windows, the island hangs over other top bars.** If an app such as MyDockFinder puts a Mac-style menu bar at the top of your screen, the island now hangs from the top edge over it, the way a MacBook's notch sits over the menu bar, instead of below it. A Windows taskbar at the top still moves the island below it. Thanks to natsutamura (#166).

**Phone drop:** the page your phone opens from the QR code no longer zooms in on a double tap. Pinch still zooms.

Everything new in 0.2.24 is here too: Claude Code and Cursor on the AI tab again. See the [0.2.24 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.24).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.24, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
