**Today shows the weeks ahead.** Signed in to Oyenza, with Google Calendar or Gmail switched on, Today has the next five weeks on its left, with a dot under each date for what's on it: a calendar event, a flight from Gmail, a bill due. On the right is your schedule from now: the meeting on now with **Join**, the rest of today, then each day that has something, to the end of the five weeks. Click a date to start the list there; click an event to open it in Google Calendar. The list scrolls with your trackpad or mouse wheel.

**Today says what's missing.** If Google Calendar is switched off for Studio, or not connected, Today says so and offers **Turn on** or **Connect**, which open Studio's switches in oyenza.app. Connect Google Calendar from there and it switches on for Studio by itself.

**One tap for the weeks.** If you switched Google Calendar or Gmail on for Studio before this version, the words you agreed to named only today, so Today shows today until you tap **Allow**, on Today itself or in oyenza.app.

**Prayer times anywhere.** Prayer times works outside Indonesia: type any of 33,000 cities, or let your location find it. Cities are looked up online as you type, so the add-on stays 1.9 MB; only the city you pick is kept. Each city gets the method its country follows, named beside it, on its own clock with summer time.

Everything new in 0.2.9 is here too: YouTube and Spotify in your browser on a Mac, Oyen as the Oyenza tab, System and Health as add-ons. See the [0.2.9 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.9).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading:** on 0.2.6 to 0.2.9, the island says **Update available** within a few hours; on Windows, **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. Or run the new installer. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
