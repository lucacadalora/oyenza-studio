**A tab for your AI plans.** The new **AI** tab, right after Focus, shows what's left of the AI coding plans on your computer: Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode Go and more. Each one gets a card with your plan, each limit as a bar with when it resets, and credits or balance left. Under **Services**, hide any of them, or add one that works with a key, such as OpenRouter.

**A heads-up before you run out.** Switch on **Tell me before I run out**, and the island tells you when a limit is nearly used up, when it runs out and when it's back. It checks every few minutes, and rests while your computer is locked, the screen is off or battery saver is on. With it off, nothing runs while the tab is closed.

**Your sign-ins stay yours.** Each one is read on your computer and goes only to its own service's server; nothing about your usage goes to Oyenza. Oyenza Studio only reads, so it can't sign you out of another app: when a sign-in has run out, the card asks you to open that app once. [What it reads, service by service](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/blob/main/docs/ai-usage.md).

**Oyenza Inference too.** Connect your inference.oyenza.com account with an 8-letter code, approved in your dashboard, and its balance and spend show beside your plans. Oyenza Studio gets a key that can only read them: it never sees your prompts, keys or payments, and can't spend your credit.

Everything new in 0.2.12 is here too: Stocks fills in as soon as you open it, and on a Mac, updates install in place. See the [0.2.12 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.12).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading:** on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12, the island says **Update available** within a few hours; on Windows, and on a Mac since 0.2.12, **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. Or run the new installer. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
