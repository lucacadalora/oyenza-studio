**New versions reach you within the hour.** Oyenza Studio now looks for a new version every hour instead of every 12, and only while someone is at the computer. **Check for updates…** sits under About in the menu (the tray and right-click menu on Windows, the menu bar on a Mac): it looks at once and tells you on the island that you're up to date, or opens the Update sheet. In Settings, **Check now** sits beside **Tell me about new versions**. And **Update available** now stays on the island until you press Update or Hide.

**Lyrics and the bar stay while a song plays.** Music playing in Zen or Firefox could lose its progress bar and its lyrics a few minutes into a song, until you paused and played again. Those browsers now and then report a song with no length; the island now keeps the length it knew and keeps counting (#140).

Everything new in 0.2.12 is here too: Stocks fills in the moment you open it, and on a Mac, updates install in place. See the [0.2.12 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.12).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading:** on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12, the island says **Update available** within a few hours; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. Or run the new installer. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
