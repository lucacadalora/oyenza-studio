**A tab for your AI plans.** The new **AI** tab, right after Focus, shows what's left of the AI coding plans on your computer: Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode Go, Gemini CLI, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and more. Each one gets a card with your plan, each limit as a bar with when it resets, and credits or balance left. Under **Services**, hide any of them, or add one that works with a key, such as OpenRouter.

**A heads-up before you run out.** Switch on **Tell me before I run out**, and the island tells you when a limit is nearly used up, when it runs out and when it's back. It checks every few minutes, and rests while your computer is locked, the screen is off or battery saver is on. With it off, nothing runs while the tab is closed.

**Your sign-ins stay yours.** Each one is read on your computer and goes only to its own service's server; nothing about your usage goes to Oyenza. Oyenza Studio only reads, so it can't sign you out of another app: when a sign-in has run out, the card asks you to open that app once. [What it reads, service by service](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/blob/main/docs/ai-usage.md). Connecting an Oyenza Inference account comes soon.

**Meeting notes (Studio Pro, Windows).** One click on the new Notes tab and Oyen listens to your meeting: your microphone and your PC's own sound together, so a call in Meet, Zoom, Teams or WhatsApp is heard with no bot joining it. Press Stop and Oyen writes the notes (summary, decisions, action items, topics) and the whole transcript, saved to Documents › Oyenza Studio › Notes. An add-on, off until you switch it on; Studio Pro is in early access.

**Only the tabs you use.** Settings has a new last row, **Tabs**: switch off Music, Today, Shelf, Tasks or Focus and it leaves the tab row. A page still opens when something asks for it, such as the Shelf when you drop a file on the island.

**OK on the prayer countdown.** In the ten minutes before a prayer, **OK** on the countdown puts it away, and the island shows your song's lyrics again until the prayer's time. The adzan still comes.

**Steps catch up sooner.** When your steps on the Health page are two hours old, not a day, it asks you to open Oyenza on your phone to update them.

**Two new add-ons, off until you switch them on.** **IELTS**: Oyen as your IELTS Speaking examiner. It opens once its server does; until then its page says Oyen is on a break. **Notifications**: WhatsApp, Telegram and Phone Link messages on the island. Windows lets only an app installed from the Microsoft Store read notifications, so it works in the Store copy.

Everything new in 0.2.15 is here too: the Mac app is signed and checked by Apple, so it opens without a trip to Privacy & Security. See the [0.2.15 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.15).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.15, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
