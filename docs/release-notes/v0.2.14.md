**The island stays open under its menu.** Right-clicking the open island used to close it as soon as the menu appeared: the pointer moved onto the menu, and the island took that as you leaving. Now it stays open while the menu is up. When the menu closes, it stays open if the pointer is back on it and closes as usual if it isn't. On Windows and on a Mac.

**New versions are always looked for.** The **Tell me about new versions** switch is gone: Oyenza Studio always looks, a couple of minutes after it starts and then every hour, and only while someone is at the computer. **Update available** still comes up on the island when there's a new version. In Settings, the row says which version you have, with **Check now** beside it. A copy that had the switch off starts looking again.

**Bluetooth pop-ups are for your headphones.** Some people saw a Bluetooth device connect and disconnect on the island, again and again, with nothing connected. Now a pop-up comes only for headphones, earbuds and speakers paired with your PC, once the change has held for 3 seconds. Devices nearby that were never paired with it stay quiet, and so does a connection that comes and goes in a moment. So do phones, mice, keyboards and watches, which come and go by themselves. On Windows; the Mac already showed only headphones.

Everything new in 0.2.13 is here too: Check for updates… in the menu, and lyrics that stay on in Zen and Firefox. See the [0.2.13 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.13).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. If you had switched off **Tell me about new versions**, your copy won't say so: run the new installer once. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
