**Stocks, on the island.** A new add-on, built in and off until you switch it on in Add-ons (the **+** at the end of the tabs): the IHSG and up to 10 stocks on the Indonesia Stock Exchange, laid out the way Stocks is on a Mac. Each row has the day's line against the previous close, the price and a change pill; click any pill to switch them all between percent, points and market cap. Pick one for its chart over **1D, 1M, 6M or 1Y** (point at the chart to read any moment) and the day's open, high and low, volume, market cap, value traded and 52-week high and low. **Add a stock** searches every listed stock by code or name; **Edit** removes and reorders them.

**Prices from oyenza.trade.** They run 10 minutes behind the exchange, like every free IDX feed, and the tab says where the market stands: open, at lunch, or closed with the day the prices are from. Oyenza Studio asks for them only while the Stocks tab is open and you're at your computer; with the add-on off, nothing is asked.

Everything new in 0.2.10 is here too: Today shows the next five weeks, and Prayer times work anywhere in the world. See the [0.2.10 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.10).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading:** on 0.2.6 to 0.2.10, the island says **Update available** within a few hours; on Windows, **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. Or run the new installer. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
