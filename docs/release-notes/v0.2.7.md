**Taps reach the app under the island (Windows touch screens).** On a touch screen, finger taps beside and below the island now reach the app under it, as mouse clicks always did. On 0.2.6 the top centre of a touch screen went dead while Oyenza Studio ran, so browser tabs and the address bar there couldn't be tapped. How the island looks and answers the mouse doesn't change.

**On a MacBook with a notch, the eyes play hide and seek.** The **Eyes** idle look used to rest inside the notch's camera, where the screen has no pixels, so it showed nothing. Now it rests beside the camera, as the clock does. The eyes (Oyen, in his theme) peek out on the side your pointer is on and look at it wherever it is on the screen. When the pointer crosses to the other side, they duck behind the camera and come out there.

Everything new in 0.2.6 is here too: the island stays on screen over maximized windows, and Oyenza Studio tells you when a new version is out. See the [0.2.6 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.6).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading:** on 0.2.6, the island says **Update available** within a few hours; on Windows, **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. Or run the new installer. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
