**Downloader (Studio Pro, early access, Windows).** Paste a link on the new **Downloads** tab, pick **Video** or **Sound**, and press **Download**: the film or its soundtrack goes to your Downloads folder and onto the Shelf. A link you copied waits as an offer when the tab opens. Each download shows how much is in, how fast and how long is left, with **Stop** at any time (it deletes the unfinished files); two run at once and the rest wait their turn. On the closed island, a small pill shows how far it is, then a **Saved** notice. [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) does the work. Switching the add-on on downloads its tools once, about 28 MB, each checked by size and SHA-256: yt-dlp and QuickJS from their own releases, and a small FFmpeg built for it. Nothing runs at rest. Not in the Microsoft Store copy. Download only what you have the right to keep.

**IELTS is part of Studio Pro.** The Add-ons tab lists IELTS, and the Downloader, only for an Oyenza account with Studio Pro. Studio Pro is in early access.

Everything new in 0.2.16 is here too: the AI tab, Meeting notes, Settings › Tabs and OK on the prayer countdown. See the [0.2.16 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.16).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.16, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
