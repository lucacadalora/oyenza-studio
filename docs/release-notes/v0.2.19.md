**Every Studio Pro feature on the Mac.** Meeting notes and Oyen's IELTS coach come to the Mac, next to the Downloader that came in 0.2.18. Studio Pro is in early access: it works for an Oyenza account that has it.

**Meeting notes on the Mac.** One click on the Notes tab and Oyen listens to your meeting, in Meet, Zoom or Teams, with no bot joining the call: your microphone, and your Mac's own sound for the other people. Press **Stop** and Oyen writes it up: a summary, the decisions, who does what, and the whole transcript, saved in Documents › Oyenza Studio › Notes. The Mac's sound needs macOS 14.2 or later (on 14.0 and 14.1 only your microphone is recorded), and macOS asks you once to allow the microphone, the sound of other apps and the Documents folder. The recording goes to Oyenza's server to be written up, and is deleted once your notes are saved. Recording a conversation can need the other people's agreement where you are: let them know you're taking notes.

**The IELTS coach on the Mac.** The IELTS tab works on the Mac now: Oyen asks IELTS Speaking questions out loud, listens to your answers through the microphone, and gives you a band, with a roast to go with it. macOS asks you once for the microphone.

**IELTS is open on Windows too.** The IELTS coach's server is live, so the IELTS tab works for Studio Pro on Windows as well, from 0.2.16 on.

Everything new in 0.2.18 and 0.2.17 is here too: the Settings window, the Downloader on Windows and the Mac, IELTS for Studio Pro and the AI tab. See the [0.2.18 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.18).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.18, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
