**YouTube and Spotify in your browser, on a Mac.** The island now shows what plays in your browser (YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify on the web) and in every other player on your Mac, not only in the Music and Spotify apps. Play/pause, next, previous and seeking from the island work for them too, with no permission prompt. Oyenza Studio reads macOS's own Now Playing, the one Control Center shows, through a small open-source helper (MediaRemote Adapter, BSD-3) that the Mac's built-in Perl runs only while something plays.

**Pill on a MacBook.** With the Pill theme on a MacBook with a notch, the island goes back into the notch when nothing is going on. Music, the timer, the prayer countdown and pop-ups float as the pill below it for as long as they show, instead of jumping up beside the camera between lines.

**Oyen is the Oyenza tab.** The tabs are now Music, Today, Shelf, Tasks, Focus and Oyenza, so Today sits beside Music. Oyenza's tab is Oyen himself: he waves each time the island opens, blinks, follows your pointer over the tabs, hops when you click him, and sleeps at night. Signed out, the tab shows **Sign in with Oyenza**.

**System and Health are add-ons, off until you switch them on.** Switch them on under **Add-ons**; there's nothing to download. Switched off, there's no System or Health tab, nothing reads your computer's counters, and nothing asks Oyenza for your steps. This update switches both off once: switch them back on if you use them. With them off, Oyenza Studio at rest on Windows uses about a third less memory and no processor time.

**Clipboard history is gone.** Pasting still works everywhere.

Everything new in 0.2.8 is here too: Today shows your day from Google Calendar and Gmail. See the [0.2.8 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.8).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading:** on 0.2.6, 0.2.7 or 0.2.8, the island says **Update available** within a few hours; on Windows, **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. Or run the new installer. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
