**The Settings window glides.** The Settings window moves now, at your screen's own rate: 120 frames a second on a MacBook Pro's ProMotion screen, 144 on a 144 Hz display. Switches slide, the sidebar's highlight glides to the section you pick, pickers and theme cards move over to your choice, and the highlight under the pointer fades in and out. A mouse wheel glides through a section, and a trackpad moves it under your fingers, momentum included. If you've switched on Reduce motion on your Mac, or switched off Animation effects in Windows, everything changes at once, as before. Each frame redraws only what moves, and nothing is drawn while nothing moves.

**The Downloader shows its progress (Studio Pro, early access).** A download reports how far it has got five times a second, so a quick YouTube video no longer goes straight from **Starting** to **Done**. Before a YouTube video's first bytes arrive, Studio has to solve YouTube's puzzle, which can take a few seconds: the row says **Solving YouTube's puzzle** while it does.

Everything new in 0.2.19 and 0.2.18 is here too: Meeting notes and the IELTS coach on the Mac, the Settings window, and the Downloader on Windows and the Mac. See the [0.2.19 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.19).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.19, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
