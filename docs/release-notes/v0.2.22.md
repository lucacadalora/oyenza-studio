**On Windows, drag the island out of your browser's tabs.** Press the closed island and drag it left or right along the top edge. It stays where you leave it, also after a restart: the spot is kept as a share of the screen's width, so it's the same spot on another monitor or scale. Opening near an edge, the island grows away from the edge, so the open island is always whole on the screen, and it goes back to its spot when it closes. To bring it back to the middle, drag it there (it snaps near the centre), or right-click it › **Move island to the centre**, a line that shows once it has moved.

One change to clicks: a click on the closed island now opens it when the button comes up instead of when it goes down, so a click can be told from a drag. Resting the pointer on it still opens it. On the Mac the island belongs at the camera, so nothing changes there.

Everything new in 0.2.21 is here too: Settings in the island again, with Advanced settings opening every setting in a window. See the [0.2.21 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.21).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.21, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
