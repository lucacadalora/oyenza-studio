**Today shows your day.** Sign in with Oyenza, right from Today, and switch on **Google Calendar** or **Gmail** for Oyenza Studio in oyenza.app (under Connected apps › Oyenza Studio). Today then shows the meeting on now, or the next one within the hour, with **Join** for a Google Meet, Zoom, Teams or Webex link. Below it come the rest of today's events, the flights Oyenza found in your booking emails, and the bills due today. Signed out, Today shows the date and the month beside a **Sign in with Oyenza** card. Oyenza Studio asks for your day when Today opens and every 5 minutes while it stays open, never otherwise, and keeps it in memory only.

**Clipboard history is an add-on, off until you switch it on.** Switch it on under **Add-ons**, or with **Clipboard history** in the right-click menu. It was on for everyone until 0.2.7, so this update switches it off once: switch it back on if you use it. Switched off, there's no Clipboard tab and nothing watches what you copy. On a Mac that also stops a check that ran twice a second.

**A Mac shows as a Mac.** Signing in now tells oyenza.app which computer it is, so Connected apps lists your Mac as a Mac, not as Windows.

Everything new in 0.2.7 is here too: on a touch screen, taps beside and below the island reach the app under it, and on a MacBook with a notch the eyes play hide and seek with the camera. See the [0.2.7 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.7).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg` and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS warns you, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

**Upgrading:** on 0.2.6 or 0.2.7, the island says **Update available** within a few hours; on Windows, **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. Or run the new installer. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
