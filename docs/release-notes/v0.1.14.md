**Paired phones: send from any network.** Pair a phone once, and it finds this PC from anywhere: the same Wi-Fi, another Wi-Fi or mobile data, whenever Oyen Poni is running. Open the Shelf, click **Phone**, then **Pair a phone**, and scan the code with your phone's camera. The phone opens drop.oyenza.com and says it's paired.

- **Keep it on the phone.** On an iPhone, tap Share › **Add to Home Screen**. On Android (Chrome or Samsung Internet), install it, and **Oyen Poni** joins the share sheet: Gallery › Share › Oyen Poni.
- **Files from your own phones go straight onto the Shelf,** with a notice on the island, the way AirDrop works between your own devices.
- **The phone can also save what's on your Shelf,** from wherever it is.
- **Paired phones** (same panel) lists each phone with **Remove**.

The phone and the PC meet through a small server, drop.oyenza.com. The files themselves go straight between them over an encrypted WebRTC link. The server never sees a file, a file name or the pairing secret, which only the QR code carries, and every message through it is signed with that secret. With no paired phone, Oyen Poni sends nothing at all.

Most networks connect directly. A few, such as some mobile and office networks, block direct links between devices. On those, connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi as the PC for now.

The phone page has Oyen Poni's look: Oyen peeks while it pairs, sits on the island once paired, carries your files while they travel and naps when the PC is away. An island at the top of the phone shows the progress and says when your files are on the Shelf.

The first time a phone connects, Windows may ask whether Oyen Poni may use the network. Allow it.

**Install:** download `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`.

**Upgrading from 0.1.x:** run the new installer. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings and data (`%APPDATA%\Poni`).

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
