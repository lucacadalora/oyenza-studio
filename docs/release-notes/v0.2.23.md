**On a Mac, drag the island along the top edge too.** On a Mac without a notch, or on another display, press the closed island and drag it left or right along the top edge, in every theme, as on Windows since 0.2.22. On a MacBook with a notch, Pill's floating island (music, the timer, pop-ups, notices and the open island) drags the same way; when it rests, it slides back into the notch, and it slides out to its spot again the next time it floats. Classic, Oyen and Glass stay at the camera there, in the menu bar, where they don't cover your browser's tabs. The island keeps its spot after a restart and opens away from the screen's edge. To bring it back to the middle, drag it there (it snaps near the centre), or pick **Move island to the centre** in the menu bar's menu or on a right-click of the island, a line that shows once it has moved.

One change to clicks, only where the island can move: a click on the closed island opens it when the button comes up instead of when it goes down, so a click can be told from a drag. Resting the pointer on it still opens it. Nothing changes on Windows.

Everything new in 0.2.22 is here too: on Windows, drag the island out of your browser's tabs. See the [0.2.22 notes](https://github.com/lucacadalora/oyenza-studio/releases/tag/v0.2.22).

**Install:** download `Oyenza-Studio-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Poni\poni.exe`. Links to `Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe` keep working: every release carries that file too.

**On a Mac:** open `Oyenza-Studio-Mac.dmg`, drag Oyenza Studio into Applications and open it. macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour on 0.2.13 to 0.2.22, and within half a day on 0.2.6 to 0.2.12; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. On a Mac, a copy older than 0.2.12 downloads the DMG instead: open it and drag Oyenza Studio into Applications. If macOS doesn't let a copy replace itself, it opens the DMG for you to do the same. Either way it upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and Oyenza sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
