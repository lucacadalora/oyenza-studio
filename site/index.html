For Windows 10 and 11

A living notch for Windows

Oyen Poni hangs a small island from the top of your screen. It shows what is happening, and opens into quick pages when you point at it: music with synced lyrics, a focus timer, a file shelf your phone can drop into, tasks, your clipboard, and money in and bills due from Oyenza.

While it rests

The island shows what's happening

Small and quiet at the top edge. It grows only when something needs a moment of your attention, then gets out of the way.

Point at it

Eight quick pages, one hover away

Point at the island or click it and it opens; move away and it closes at once. Scroll over the header to switch pages; the gear on the right opens Settings. Sign in to Oyenza and a ninth page joins them.

Music, with the words

Controls for whatever is playing: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube in your browser, or any app that shows up in Windows' media controls. Lyrics scroll in time with the song, from the free LRCLIB library, or Apple Music's own synced lyrics if you have a subscription. YouTube videos find theirs too, even when the title carries subtitle tags or puts the song name first. After an intro or a break, the waiting dots fold away the moment the next line starts.

New in 0.1.7

Read along in Korean, Japanese and Chinese

Like Apple Music's Show Pronunciation, each word of a lyric line gets its reading in Latin letters, set small underneath: the syllables for Korean, romaji for Japanese and pinyin with tone marks for Chinese. Since 0.1.12 every Japanese line gets romaji on any PC: Oyen Poni reads kanji with its own built-in dictionary, with nothing to install and nothing leaving your PC. Turn it off in Settings or the tray menu.

Focus

A Pomodoro timer that keeps counting in the island.

Health

Today's steps against your goal, and your week, from Apple Health or Health Connect on your phone through Oyenza. Signing in works now; the steps come in a later version.

Shelf

Drop files on the island to keep them handy, or send them from your phone. Click to open, or drag them back out.

Tasks

A tiny to-do list for the things you'd otherwise forget.

Clipboard

Recent copied text, kept in memory only. It skips anything a password manager marks as private.

Today

The date and calendar, plus CPU, memory, disk and battery at a glance. Click one to open it on the System page.

New in 0.1.8

Phone drop: files from your phone, onto the Shelf

Open the Shelf, click Phone and scan the QR code with your phone's camera. Pick photos or files, tap Send, and the island asks before anything arrives. It works with iPhone and Android over your own Wi-Fi: nothing to install on the phone, and nothing goes through the internet. The phone can save what's on your Shelf too.

New in 0.1.10

Settings, right in the island

Press the gear beside the pin. Your Oyenza account comes first: sign in, see who is signed in, or disconnect. Below it are the theme, the idle look, and switches for lyrics, pronunciation, sounds, clipboard history, full-screen apps and starting with Windows. Changes apply at once.

New in 0.1.11

System: your PC at a glance

CPU, memory, drives, network and battery, each with the last minute as a chart, the apps using the most, and Task Manager one click away. It reads only while the page is open, from the same counters Task Manager uses: no driver, no admin rights, and nothing leaves your PC.

Optional theme · New look in 0.1.7

Oyen takes over the island

The island stays black and quiet by default. Switch on the Oyen theme and the whole island turns Oyen: warm cream, dark brown text, his orange on the controls, and Oyen himself peeking over the edge, the same Oyen as in the Oyenza app.

  • Cream and brown on every page, with Oyen's orange on the play button, the progress and volume bars and the switches.
  • Every Latin letter is set in Outfit, this website's typeface, which now ships inside the app.
  • Built from the Oyenza logo, so he stays sharp at every size and scaling. On an open page he peeks up beside the pin, eyes on your cursor.
  • He moves only when something happens and sleeps after midnight. Oyen Poni still uses no CPU while nothing moves.

Open the island and press the gear, then Theme › Oyen. Switch back to Classic any time.

Oyenza account · New in 0.1.9

Your Oyenza, at the top of your screen

Sign in with your oyenza.app account the way you sign in on a TV: press the gear, choose Sign in with Oyenza, and approve the code the island shows at oyenza.app/connect. No password is ever typed into Oyen Poni. An Oyenza page then joins the island.

  • Money as it arrives. When Oyenza logs money coming in, the island tells you, with the amount and sender hidden until you click Show.
  • Bills before they're due. Subscriptions and card due dates for the next 7 days, and a reminder the day before each one.
  • Note a spend in a second. Type "kopi 25rb" on the Oyenza page and press Enter: it goes straight to Oyenza, with 10 seconds to undo.
  • Your steps, next. Steps from Apple Health or Health Connect will come through Oyenza in a later version.

Signing in is optional. This PC gets a key of its own, never your password: it sees money coming in and bills due, and saves the spends you type, nothing else. Disconnect it in the island's Settings, or in oyenza.app under Connected apps.

Private by design

Your data stays on your PC

No account needed

No sign-up and no tracking. Tasks, shelf and focus history are files in your own user folder.

Clipboard in memory

Copied text is never written to disk, and anything a password manager marks private is skipped.

Smooth and light

Drawn on your graphics card in step with your screen's refresh rate (60, 144 or 240 Hz). Native Rust, no web view, and no CPU while nothing moves.

Install

Ready in under a minute

  1. Download the installer

    Oyen-Poni-Setup.exe, from the latest release on GitHub. A portable zip is on the Releases page too.

  2. Run it

    It installs just for you and needs no admin rights.

  3. If Windows warns you

    Builds aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway. If Smart App Control is on, Windows won't run unsigned apps at all; a Microsoft Store version is on the way.
