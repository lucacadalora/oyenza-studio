Oyenza Studio is a free, open-source island at the top of the screen, for Windows and Mac. If you make videos about desk setups, Windows tips, music, study or games, film Studio on your own computer. We pay for every 1,000 views your video gets.

How it works

  1. Email hello@oyenza.com with a link to your channel. We reply with the brief and your own link, like studio.oyenza.com/yourname.
  2. Install Studio on your computer and use it for a few days, so you know what you want to show.
  3. Film it running on your real screen, with your own face or voice. Say your link and show it on screen.
  4. Post on TikTok, YouTube or Instagram with the platform's paid-partnership label on, then send us the video's link.
  5. Thirty days later we count the views and pay you.

The rules

Every paid video follows these:

  • You're 18 or older.
  • You film Studio on your own Windows PC or Mac, with your own face or voice.
  • You mark it as an ad: switch on the platform's paid-partnership label, and show or say "Ad" in the first seconds.
  • Studio's name and your link stay readable on screen for at least 3 seconds.
  • You show only what Studio really does.
  • You never recommend a stock or promise returns, even when you show the Stocks add-on.
  • No bought views, bots or reposted copies. Those videos aren't paid, and the account leaves the program.
  • The video stays public for at least 90 days.

Who can join

Any adult with a public TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account and a computer that runs Studio: Windows 10 or 11, or macOS 14 or later.

Your follower count doesn't matter. We pay for views, so a small account with a good video earns the same rate as a big one. You can make more than one video; each one is paid on its own views.

Joining is free. We will never ask you for money. If someone does, it isn't us.

What your link counts

Your link counts the visits and download taps that come from your video, without cookies, so we can see which formats work. It never tells us who your viewers are.
