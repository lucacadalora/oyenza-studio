# Oyenza Studio privacy

Oyenza Studio is made by Jatevo Labs. It needs no account, and has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Unless you sign in to Oyenza, it never sends us anything about you: its only request to our website reads one public file, to see whether a new version is out.

## What leaves your PC

**Lyrics lookups.** When a song plays and **Synced lyrics** is on, Oyenza Studio asks [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net), a free public lyrics library, for that song's lyrics. The request carries the song's title, artist, album and length, plus a User-Agent that names Oyenza Studio and links to this project. LRCLIB is run by other people and has its own terms. Answers are cached on your PC, so a song isn't looked up again every time it plays.

To stop lookups, right-click the island or the tray icon and turn off **Synced lyrics**.

**Apple Music lyrics, only if you turn them on.** If you add your Apple Music token (right-click the island, then **Apple Music lyrics › Use token from clipboard**), Oyenza Studio also asks Apple Music for lyrics the way Apple's web player does. It checks the token, searches Apple Music's catalog for the playing song's artist and title, and downloads that song's lyrics. These requests go to Apple (`music.apple.com` and `amp-api.music.apple.com`) and carry your token; the token goes nowhere else. To stop, choose **Remove token** in the same menu.

**Phone drop, only while its panel is open.** When you open Phone drop on the Shelf, Oyenza Studio serves a small web page on your local network so your phone can send files to the Shelf and save Shelf files. Only a device on the same network with that session's secret link, which only the QR code carries, can open it. Files go straight between your phone and your PC: nothing passes through the internet or through us. Oyenza Studio reads nothing from an upload until you click **Accept**. The page stops working when you click **Done**, or after 10 minutes without a phone.

**Paired phones, only after you pair one.** **Pair a phone** (Shelf › Phone) shows a QR code with a pairing secret made on your PC. The phone that scans it keeps the secret, and so does your PC, sealed so only your Windows account can read it. From then on, while Oyenza Studio runs, it keeps one request open at our small meeting server, `drop.oyenza.com`, so the phone can find the PC from any network. The server sees your PC's and phone's IP addresses, when they are online, and an address for the pair that is derived from the secret; it never sees the secret itself, a device name, a file name or a file. Every message through it is signed with the secret, so it can't pretend to be your phone or your PC. Files then go straight between phone and PC, encrypted by WebRTC with keys made on the two devices. When both networks block a direct link, they pass through a relay run by Cloudflare that forwards the encrypted bytes and can't read them. To find its own internet address, Oyenza Studio also asks a public STUN server (Cloudflare's or Google's) when a phone connects. **Remove** a phone in **Paired phones** and the pair stops working on both sides; with no paired phones, nothing is sent at all.

**Oyenza, only if you sign in.** Signing in (the gear on the open island, then **Sign in with Oyenza**; or right-click the island, then **Oyenza › Sign in to Oyenza…**; on a Mac, also the menu bar under Oyen's mark) connects this computer to your [oyenza.app](https://oyenza.app) account: you approve a code in oyenza.app, and Oyenza Studio gets a key of its own. It then asks `api.oyenza.app`, about once a minute while you use the PC (every 3 minutes on battery, never while it's locked or asleep), for money that came in or went out by itself and bills due in the next 30 days; every 15 seconds while the Oyenza tab is open, for the last 7 days of transactions it lists; every 15 minutes while you use the PC, and when the Health tab opens, for your steps (only while the Health add-on is on); when the Today tab opens and every 5 minutes while it stays open, for your day; and it sends the spends you type in the island. It sends its key, its version and your PC's UTC offset with each request, and the PC's name once, when you sign in. Of your money, the key reads only those 7 days of transactions (titles, sums, categories, wallets and times, in the space where Oyenza logs your bank's emails and notifications), never balances, account numbers, notes or receipts, and it can't change or delete anything else or touch your account. Your steps reach the PC only if you turn on **Show on Oyenza Studio** under your steps in Health (Sehat) in Oyenza on your iPhone or Android: then the PC receives the daily totals of steps, distance and active energy for the last 7 days, with your step goal and when your phone read them, and keeps them in memory only, never on disk or in its log. Sleep, heart rate and weight never reach the PC. Turning the switch off deletes Oyenza's copy, and the PC drops its own the next time it asks (within 15 minutes, or when the Health tab opens); switching the Health add-on off drops the PC's copy at once. Your day reaches the PC only for the apps you switch on for Today in oyenza.app (Connected apps › Oyenza Studio): with Google Calendar, today's events on your main calendar (title, times, place and one meeting link, only from Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Webex); with Gmail, today's flights that Oyenza already read from your booking emails. Oyenza reads them when the PC asks and doesn't keep them; the PC keeps them in memory only. Guests, event notes, other calendars and your emails never reach the PC. Oyenza's own privacy page covers what happens there: [oyenza.com/privasi](https://oyenza.com/privasi). **Disconnect** in Settings (or **Oyenza › Disconnect** in the menu) signs this PC out and removes the key and everything Oyenza Studio kept from Oyenza; you can also disconnect it in oyenza.app.

**Add-ons, only the ones you switch on.** Add-ons (the last tab, a "+") are extra features whose files aren't part of the app. Switching one on (in that tab, or Japanese readings from the offer under a Japanese song's lyrics) downloads its files from this project's releases on GitHub (`github.com`, which serves them from `objects.githubusercontent.com`), the same way your browser downloads the installer. Nothing about you goes with the request beyond what any download carries (your IP address and a User-Agent naming Oyenza Studio). Each file is checked against the size and fingerprint built into the app and refused if it differs. Switching the add-on off deletes its files. While every add-on is off, nothing is downloaded.

**New versions.** About two minutes after it starts and then every hour, Oyenza Studio reads one small file, `studio.oyenza.com/latest.json`, which names the latest version (never while the PC is locked, the screen is off or battery saver is on), and again whenever you pick **Check for updates…** in the menu or **Check now** in Settings. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The request carries nothing about you beyond what any page view carries (your IP address, and a User-Agent naming Oyenza Studio and its version), and we get no record of it. The file is signed, and the app ignores it unless the signature checks out. On Windows, once the file names a newer version, the app downloads that version's Setup from this project's releases on GitHub, once, the way add-ons download, and keeps it in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Poni\update` until you install it; it is deleted once you run that version. On a Mac it does the same with the DMG, in `~/Library/Caches/Poni/update`, with the app it takes out of it.

**Prayer times' location, only while that add-on is on.** To pick the nearest kab/kota or city, Oyenza Studio asks Windows where the PC is, through Windows' own location service (Settings › Privacy & security › Location decides whether it may). It asks when the add-on starts and again every few hours, so a trip moves the place. The point is used on your PC to choose the nearest place, then dropped: it isn't saved and Oyenza Studio sends it nowhere. Kemenag's kab/kota are in the add-on; cities outside Indonesia are looked up online, a small file at a time, from `studio.oyenza.com/places/` (the website, hosted by GitHub Pages). Away from Kemenag's places, the location asks for the cities of the 5-degree squares (about 550 km across) around it; typing a place asks for the cities whose names start with the first two letters typed. Those requests carry nothing about you beyond what any page view carries (your IP address, and a User-Agent naming Oyenza Studio and its version), and we get no record of them. Only the chosen place is saved, in your settings: its name, point and clock. Picking a place by hand stops the asking.

**Stocks, only while that add-on is on and its tab is open.** The Stocks tab shows the IHSG and the stocks on your list with prices from [oyenza.trade](https://oyenza.trade), Oyenza's stock market site. While the tab is open, and never otherwise (not while the PC is locked or the screen is off), Oyenza Studio asks `oyenza.trade` for those prices: when the tab opens, then about every 15 seconds while the Indonesia Stock Exchange trades, every minute around its sessions and every 10 minutes while it's shut. The request carries the codes on your list and nothing about you beyond what any page view carries (your IP address, and a User-Agent naming Oyenza Studio and its version). The first time the search opens it downloads the list of every listed stock's code and name, and picking 1M, 6M or 1Y downloads that stock's daily closes for the year, each once while the app runs. Your list is saved with your tasks; the prices are kept in memory only. Switch Stocks off in Add-ons to stop.

Nothing else is sent anywhere. Music, volume, Bluetooth battery levels, the date and calendar, and the system stats are all read from Windows on your PC.

**Show over games reads Windows' frame log, only while it's on and a game is in front.** To count frames per second, Oyenza Studio reads Windows' own log of the frames apps hand to the screen (event tracing, as PresentMon and Xbox Game Bar do) and keeps only the full-screen game's: when each frame came and which window it was for, the last two seconds of them, in memory. Nothing is loaded into the game, nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere. The one-time OK adds your Windows account to Windows' Performance Log Users group, which lets programs you run read Windows' performance logs; remove your account from that group to undo it (see the README).

**The Glass theme reads the screen behind the island, only while it's on.** To bend what's under the island, Oyenza Studio copies the part of your screen behind its window (the island and the space it opens into) whenever something there changes. It doesn't copy while the island is hidden, while a full-screen app is in front, or while the PC is locked. The copy stays in your PC's memory and on its graphics card, and each new copy replaces the last. It is never saved, never shown anywhere else and never sent anywhere. While Glass is on, Windows leaves Oyenza Studio out of screenshots, screen recordings and screen sharing. Switch to Classic or Oyen to stop both.

## What stays on your PC

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Settings, tasks, focus count, your Stocks list and the shelf's list of files (the files themselves stay where they are), and any drinks logged by the Water page of older versions | `%APPDATA%\Poni\` |
| Lyrics cache and a log file (the log names songs that were looked up, to help fix problems) | `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Poni\` |
| Your Apple Music token, if you added one, sealed so only your Windows account on this PC can read it | `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Poni\` |
| Your Oyenza sign-in (this PC's key and your account's masked email), if you signed in, sealed the same way (on a Mac, a file only your Mac account can read, in `~/Library/Application Support/Poni/`). What Oyenza showed, your steps and your day too, is kept in memory only | `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Poni\` |
| The Glass theme's copy of the screen behind the island, only while Glass is on | Memory only (the PC's and its graphics card's), replaced by each new copy, never written to disk |
| Paired phones (their pairing secrets and names), sealed so only your Windows account on this PC can read them | `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Poni\` |
| Files of the add-ons you switched on (Prayer times: the place list and the adzan recording; Japanese readings: the reading dictionary), deleted when you switch the add-on off | `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Poni\addons\` |
| Files you accepted from your phone with Phone drop, and files your paired phones sent | `Downloads\Oyenza Studio\`, kept when you uninstall |
| What Oyenza Studio Recorder tells the island about its recording (starting, recording, paused, saving and its length) and that it has spoken to the island once. The messages go from one app to the other on this PC, and the videos stay the recorder's | Memory only, and one flag in `%APPDATA%\Poni\` |

Uninstalling removes the app, its cache, its log, the add-ons' files, the Apple Music token, the paired phones and the Oyenza sign-in (the key also stops working on Oyenza's side after 90 days unused, or at once if you disconnect it in oyenza.app). If you installed from the Microsoft Store, Windows also removes the files the app created. Data from an earlier copy installed from GitHub stays in `%APPDATA%\Poni\` until you delete it.

## Microsoft Store

If you install from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download and updates under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement), and gives publishers summary reports such as install counts and crash reports.
